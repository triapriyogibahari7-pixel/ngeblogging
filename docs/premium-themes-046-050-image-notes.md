## Asset status
Ten external reference images are linked through img elements. URLs, source pages, dimensions, and alt descriptions are stored in imageSources metadata; visible captions link to source pages. References are not owned assets and have not been cleared for commercial production. Attribution is not a license. Original authorship, model/property permissions, permitted hosting/delivery, and provenance require review. Some stock imagery may be generated; its origin has not been verified. Do not remove stock watermarks or claim the pictured work as the fictional brands' own.

The fashion images are third-party editorial references, not the fictional label's campaign. Landscape images do not establish an offered destination or safe route. Pottery images do not establish a maker's identity or food-contact suitability. Watch photos show third-party products, including a Chopard reference; no endorsement, affiliation, authenticity assessment, or specification transfer is claimed. The secondary watch is a different object. Dining references do not depict an actual Saji venue.

## Delivery and testing
Images use width/height, alt text, no-referrer, eager primary loading, and lazy secondary loading. Actual delivery, cropping, and rights are unverified. There is no self-hosted image pipeline, responsive srcset, or automatic image-error recovery. Remote assets can fail or change. Replace with owned or appropriately licensed optimized images before production.

No build, browser rendering, responsive screenshots, contrast audit, keyboard audit, performance measurement, or image-network validation has been run. CSS breakpoints are implementation only, not proof of passing responsive tests. All five themes remain independent prototypes, not production-ready premium releases.
